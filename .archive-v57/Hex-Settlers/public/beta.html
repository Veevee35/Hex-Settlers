Hex Settlers is a free browser-based settlement strategy game with classic and seafaring maps, 4–8-player matches, AI opponents, ranked play, custom rules and daily/weekly challenges.

We're recruiting players who want to explore the game, try multiplayer with friends, and tell us what breaks. No installation required; desktop and mobile browsers supported. The beta is free and a work in progress.

Join an organized test

  1. Create a free account or log in with a supported sign-in provider.
  2. Try the optional interactive tutorial, then play a complete match on mobile or desktop.
  3. During scheduled sessions, join a public lobby or invite friends to a private one. Aim to finish the match.
  4. Tap the draggable insect icon on any game page to describe crashes, problems, balance concerns or ideas. Include the steps that reproduce it.
  5. Check back for new challenge maps and another playtest session. Early contributors can earn a Founding Playtester profile badge (first 150 eligible).

For focused load tests, coordinated participation matters more than total registrations. If no session is listed yet, solo challenges and private games are still available.
